Each bar shows a barley variety's total yield. Colored segments show the
contribution from each growing site; the horizontal legend identifies them.

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

The barley table is loaded from the versioned Vega datasets collection.
GenomeSpy sums yields by variety and site, then stacks the totals in the browser.

Adapted from the [official GenomeSpy example](https://genomespy.app/playground/?spec=/docs/example-specs/core/legends/horizontal-stacked-bar.json).
:::
