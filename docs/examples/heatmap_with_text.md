Zoom in with the mouse wheel to reveal the numbers inside the cells. Text
switches between white and black to stay readable against the tile color.
The zoom hint fades away as the cells become large enough to read.

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

This example adapts GenomeSpy's
[heatmap with text](https://genomespy.app/playground/?spec=/docs/example-specs/core/layout/layer/heatmap_with_text.json).
NumPy creates positions for a 200 × 200 grid. GenomeSpy calculates the grid
coordinates and synthetic wave pattern in the browser. The gallery loads
the positions from an Arrow file.
:::
