The first six panels show marker expression: darker purple means higher
expression. They use the same color scale. The last two panels show total counts
(`n_counts`) and cell types, with separate legends.

Zoom with the mouse wheel and drag to pan; all eight panels move together.
Hover over a dot for the cell and its annotation or expression value.
Compare with the [cell-type UMAP](pbmc_umap.md).

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

2,638 cells from Scanpy's processed
[10x Genomics PBMC3k dataset](https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets/3-k-pbm-cs-from-a-healthy-donor-1-standard-1-1-0)
(CC BY 4.0), retaining its saved UMAP coordinates, log-transformed counts, and
cell-type labels.
:::
