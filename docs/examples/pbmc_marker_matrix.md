Color shows the **average log-transformed counts** for each cell type and
marker gene. Bars at the left show cell counts. Hover over a tile for its exact
value.

Colored blocks above the genes identify their associated cell types. The tree
at the right groups cell types with similar expression.

Explore individual cells in the [heatmap](pbmc_cell_heatmap.md) or
[expression tracks](pbmc_expression_tracks.md).

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

12 markers from 2,638 cells in Scanpy's processed
[10x Genomics PBMC3k dataset](https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets/3-k-pbm-cs-from-a-healthy-donor-1-standard-1-1-0)
(CC BY 4.0), retaining its log-transformed counts and cell-type labels.
:::
