Drag a rectangle over the scatter plot to select penguins. Selected points
keep their species colors, and the bar charts count the selection by species
and recorded sex. Before a selection is made, the summaries include all rows.

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

The Palmer Penguins table is loaded from the versioned Vega datasets
collection. Source: Gorman KB, Williams TD, Fraser WR (2014),
[doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0090081](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090081).
GenomeSpy filters and counts the selected rows in the browser.

Adapted from the [official GenomeSpy example](https://genomespy.app/playground/?spec=/docs/example-specs/docs/grammar/parameters/penguins.json).
:::
