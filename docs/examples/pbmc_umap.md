Each dot is a cell, colored by cell type. Use the mouse wheel to zoom and drag
to pan. Hover over a dot to identify the cell and its type.

Compare where genes are expressed in the [UMAP panels](pbmc_umap_genes.md).

:::{admonition} Data use and provenance
:class: note

2,638 cells from Scanpy's processed
[10x Genomics PBMC3k dataset](https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets/3-k-pbm-cs-from-a-healthy-donor-1-standard-1-1-0)
(CC BY 4.0), retaining its saved UMAP coordinates, log-transformed counts, and
cell-type labels.
:::

Inspired by [Scanpy's UMAP examples](https://scanpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/plotting/core.html#visualization-of-gene-expression-and-other-variables).
